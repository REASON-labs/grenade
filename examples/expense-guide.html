    Step 3 of 6

    Every expense needs a receipt, a category and a reason

    • Photo of the receipt, taken with your phone in Ramp.

    • Category, picked from the list.

    • One line on why you spent it.

    • Without a receipt, you won't be reimbursed.

    Example: you pay on November 3 and upload on November 5. You get it back on the November 15 payroll.

    Step 4 of 6

    If you paid with your own money, upload it in Ramp to get it back

    • Upload within 30 days of paying.

    • Choose "Reimbursement" as the type.

    • You get paid in the next payroll: the 15th or the last day of the month.

    • The step 3 rules apply here too: receipt, category, reason.
